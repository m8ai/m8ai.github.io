How I work

I've spent the last 15 years in the messy middle of enterprise IT, where ambitious data strategy and digital transformations meet legacy systems, old processes and competing stakeholders. My job is making the two sides meet without anyone's Outlook exploding.

That means running scrum meetings with the team in the morning and reviewing data architecture with engineers in the afternoon. I care more about whether a reporting landscape survives and is easy to maintain than whether its architecture diagram looks clean in the deck.

Where I add the most value

Leadership & Delivery

Planning, governing and delivering complex end-to-end analytics and IT programs — requirements, architecture, compliance and team leadership, using both classic and agile methods.

Data engineering & BI platforms

Hands-on delivery across SAP, SQL, PowerBI and Qlik — from data model to the dashboard someone actually opens every morning.

Operability & maintainability

I design analytics and cloud solutions with an eye on what happens after go-live — automated, well-documented, and simple enough for a lean team to run without me standing next to it.
